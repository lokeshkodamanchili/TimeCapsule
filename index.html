About TimeCapsule

Unlock the Past, Ace the Future. Connecting academic experience with the next generation.

TimeCapsule – Unlock the Past, Ace the Future is an academic knowledge-sharing platform developed to preserve valuable study materials from seniors and make them accessible to juniors.

The system allows seniors to upload notes, PDFs, assignments, and important resources, which are securely stored and organized using Microsoft Excel, SharePoint, and Power Automate.

The platform ensures structured document storage, automated workflows, and smooth knowledge transfer between batches, helping students prepare smarter and achieve better academic success.

User Guide

Follow these steps to contribute resources or request access to academic study materials.

👨‍🎓 For Seniors

Login with college mail only (Aditya college)
01. Open the Senior Upload Form.
02. Fill in subject details and upload your document.
03. Submit the form for approval.
04. Receive an email notification (Approved / Rejected).
05. Approved files are stored securely in subject-wise folders.

👨‍🎓 For Juniors

Login with college mail only (Aditya college)
01. Open the Junior Access Form.
02. Submit your request with required details.
03. Receive the SharePoint folder link via email.
04. Access study materials and submit feedback.

🔄 System Process

✔ Documents are routed for approval through Power Automate.
✔ Approved files are stored in SharePoint.
✔ All activities are logged and tracked for transparency.
